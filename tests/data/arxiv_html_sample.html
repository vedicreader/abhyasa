3 Scaled Dot-Product Attention

The input consists of queries and keys of dimension dkd_{k}. We compute the dot products of the query with all keys, and we scale the dot products by 1dk\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}} before the softmax. A price of $5 is not maths.

A\mathrm{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\mathrm{softmax}(\frac{QK^{T}}{\sqrt{d_{k}}})V (1)

We suspect that for large values of d, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax function into regions where it has extremely small gradients.
